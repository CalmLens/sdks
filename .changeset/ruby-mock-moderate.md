Breaking: `CalmLens::MockServerClient` takes a `moderate:` callable in place of `queued_outcome:` and `analyzed_outcome:`. It receives a `CalmLens::MockModerationInput` with the stored asset, its content (`bytes`, and `text` for plain-text uploads), the trigger (`upload`, `retry`, `rerun` or `complete`) and whether CalmLens would queue the work, and returns a status or a decision built with `CalmLens::MockDecision.of(status)`, whose `flag(category, score, qualifiers)` and `not_evaluated(category)` fill in the asset's full moderation report, checking every category, qualifier and score. Uploads now add `asset.uploaded`, `asset.classified` and the automated `asset.<status>` or `asset.failed` events. `complete_processing(asset_id, decision = nil)` finishes a pending asset as CalmLens would. `fail_next_request` fails one request at a time, takes `details:` and `retry_after:` for API errors and rejects unknown operations; the mock's `max_retries:` defaults to 0. Recorded requests carry their response `status`, their paths have decoded segments, and `clear_requests` forgets them. `enqueue_event` with an `asset_id:` attaches the asset's snapshot, and `decide` refuses `pending` and `failed`.
