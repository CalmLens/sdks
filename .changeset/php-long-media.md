Long audio and video: `$client->assets->listTimeline(['assetId' => ...])` pages through every segment of an audio or video asset's findings timeline in time order, from `GET /projects/{projectId}/assets/{assetId}/timeline` with the client's project ID; pass each page's opaque `nextCursor` as `cursor`. `classification.moderation.timeline` (`segmentCount`, or `null` without a timeline) replaces `classification.moderation.raw.timeline`. `classification.progress` reports `completedSections` and `totalSections` while long media is analysed in sections, the `asset.processing-progress` event reports the same, and the failure reasons include `media-limit` and `spend-limit`.

Assets describe their files directly: `content.image` is replaced by `content.width`, `height`, `durationSeconds`, `pageCount`, `perceptualHash` and `perceptualHashQuality`, plus `content.rendered` (`kind`, `format`, `width`, `height` and, for audio, `peaks`), the displayable file, and `content.preview` (`width`, `height`, `thumbhash`, `averageColor`), a still image for the asset; either is `null` when the asset has none. `content.fileMetadata` is renamed `content.metadata`.

The CDN serves a new `preview` resource, and `Cdn::SIGNED_RESOURCES` lists the resources a signed URL can be created for: `original`, `rendered` and `preview` (`Cdn::RESOURCES` adds `placeholder`). Lenses and transforms apply to `preview`, and to `rendered` when it is an image; the client refuses them on `original` and `placeholder`, and the CDN refuses them on a rendered PDF, video or audio file with `asset/invalid-transform`.

`MockServerClient` serves the timeline at the project path (always empty, refusing any cursor with `validation/invalid-cursor`, and a safety-held asset with `asset/safety-held`), gives mock assets a `null` progress and moderation timeline, and reports each asset type's rendered file and preview as the API does, at upload and once decided. Removing or purging content clears `rendered`, `preview`, `extractedText` and `metadata`; a safety hold withholds the rendered file and preview and makes every CDN resource, the placeholder included, return 403 `asset/not-available`. Its CDN serves the upload as the rendered file (with the rendered format as `Content-Type`) and as an image's preview, refuses a resource the asset doesn't have with 404 `asset/no-rendered-version`, and refuses a signed URL for any other resource with `validation/invalid-value`.
