`MockCalmLensServerClient` now decides uploads through a `moderate` function in `mock_options`, which replaces `queued_outcome` and `analyzed_outcome`. It receives a `MockModerationInput` with the stored asset, its content (the uploaded `bytes`, and the `text` of a plain-text upload), the `trigger` (`upload`, `retry`, `rerun` or `complete`) and whether the work is `queued`, and returns a status or a decision built with `mock_decision(status).flag(category, score, qualifiers).not_evaluated(category)`, which fills the asset's report with every category, its score and the policy's `flaggedCategories` as CalmLens does. Without it, queued uploads, retries and reruns stay pending and everything else is allowed. `complete_processing(asset_id, decision=None)` finishes a pending asset as the pipeline would. Uploads add `asset.uploaded`, then `asset.classified` and the automated `asset.<status>`, or `asset.failed`, to the events feed. `fail_next_request` takes `details` and `retry_after` for API errors, fails one request per queued failure and refuses unknown operations. Recorded requests carry their decoded `path` and response `status`, and `clear_requests()` forgets them. `decide` refuses `pending` and `failed`.
