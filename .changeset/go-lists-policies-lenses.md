Every list pages and sorts the same way, policies are an organisation resource, and lenses can be managed one by one.

- List options take `Sort`, a comma-separated list of fields descending when prefixed with `-` (such as `"-createdAt,id"`), in place of `Ordering`; the `Order` type and its `OrderAscending` and `OrderDescending` constants are removed. Every page has `Items`, `NextCursor` and `HasMore` (folders also return `Ancestors`), without `PageSize` and `Ordering`. `ListAssetsOptions` filters by folder through `Filter` (`folderId==fldr_…`, or `folderId==null` for the root) and no longer has `FolderID`; `SearchAssetsOptions` takes `PageSize` in place of `Limit`.
- Every list has an iterator: `Folders.ListAll`, `HashLists.ListAll`, `HashLists.ListEntriesAll`, `Policies.ListAll`, `Lenses.ListAll` and `Assets.ListTimelineAll`, beside `Assets.ListAll` and `Assets.ListChildrenAll`. They return the generic `calmlens.Iterator[T]`, with `Next`, `Item`, `Err` and `All`; `AssetIterator` builds on it and keeps `Asset` and `Page`.
- Policies are read and changed by ID for the API key's organisation: `Policies.List(ctx, ListPoliciesOptions{...})` returns a `PolicyPage`, `Policies.RetrieveAssigned` returns the project's policy, and `Policies.Reset` and `Policies.Upgrade` replace `Policies.ApplyPreset`. A policy's `Preset.Mode` (`PolicyPresetModeLive`, `PolicyPresetModePinned` or `PolicyPresetModeCopy`) replaces `Preset.Own` and `Policy.AutomaticUpdates`. Policy settings define every category with its `Action`, `Allowances` and `Restrictions` (nil rule lists are sent as empty lists), rules no longer have `Match`, and a decision's settings snapshot names the default action `Action` in place of `CategoryAction`.
- `Lenses.List`, `Lenses.ListAll`, `Lenses.Create`, `Lenses.Retrieve`, `Lenses.Update` and `Lenses.Delete` manage a project's lenses. Lenses have no `Version`: `EnsureLens` and `EnsureLenses` change a lens to the definition sent, and the CDN serves the new definition. `CodeLensNotFound` and `CodeLensIdentifierTaken` are added and `CodeLensVersionConflict` is removed.
- Uploads send `wait` in place of `queue`; `UploadAssetPartOptions.Wait` replaces `Queue` to override the choice saved when the upload started. `SignUploadTokenOptions.ExpiresIn` replaces `ExpiresInSeconds`.
- `Asset.Visibility` is always set and is a `string`. Moderation fields are nil until moderation decides, `Raw.HashMatches` is always a list, OCR's text regions are `Classification.OCR.TextRegions`, and feature results no longer include `Stage` (the `FeatureStage` constants are removed).
- `calmlens.GetEventFact(event, key)` and `calmlens.GetEventFacts(event)` read an event's facts. `FactReviewerName` and `AssetDecisionFacts.ReviewerName` are removed: webhooks and the events feed never deliver a reviewer's name.
- The `calmlenstest` mock follows all of these.
