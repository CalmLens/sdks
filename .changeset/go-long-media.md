Long audio and video, rendered media and previews.

- `Assets.ListTimeline(ctx, ListAssetTimelineOptions{AssetID, Cursor, PageSize})` pages through every segment of an audio or video asset's findings timeline in time order, from `GET /v1/projects/{projectId}/assets/{assetId}/timeline` in the client's project. Pass each page's `NextCursor` back unchanged; cursors are opaque.
- `Classification.Moderation.Timeline` is a `*ModerationTimeline` with `SegmentCount` for an audio or video asset with a findings timeline, and nil otherwise. `ModerationRaw` no longer has a `Timeline`, and the `MediaClassificationReport` type is removed.
- `Classification.Progress` reports `CompletedSections` and `TotalSections` while long media is analysed in sections, and the `asset.processing-progress` event (`EventAssetProcessingProgress`) reports the same. The failure reasons include `media-limit` and `spend-limit` (`AssetFailureReasonMediaLimit`, `AssetFailureReasonSpendLimit`).
- `Content.Rendered` (`*AssetRendered`) describes the asset's displayable file: its `Kind` (`AssetRenderedKindImage`, `AssetRenderedKindPDF`, `AssetRenderedKindVideo` or `AssetRenderedKindAudio`), `Format`, `Width`, `Height` and, for audio, waveform `Peaks`. `Content.Preview` (`*AssetPreview`) describes its still image: `Width`, `Height`, `Thumbhash` and `AverageColor`. They replace `Content.Image` and the `AssetImage` type. The file's own `Width`, `Height`, `DurationSeconds`, `PageCount`, `PerceptualHash` and `PerceptualHashQuality` are on `Content`, and `Content.FileMetadata` is now `Content.Metadata`.
- The CDN's new `preview` resource (`ResourcePreview`) serves the asset's still image: the image itself, a document's first page or slide, a website's first screen, a video's poster frame or an audio waveform. `CDN.GetAssetURL`, `CDN.Serve` and `Assets.CreateSignedURL` accept it, and it takes lenses and transforms. The `rendered` resource takes them only when it is an image; the CDN answers `asset/invalid-transform` otherwise, and `asset/no-rendered-version` for a resource the asset doesn't have. `original` and `placeholder` take no lens or transform. `SignedCDNResources` lists the resources a signed URL can be created for.
- The mock client serves the timeline endpoint (empty, as the mock analyses no media, and `asset/safety-held` while a safety hold is on), fills `Content.Rendered` and `Content.Preview` for each asset type, serves the `preview` and `rendered` resources as the CDN does, refuses every resource of a safety-held asset, and gives mock assets a nil progress and timeline.
