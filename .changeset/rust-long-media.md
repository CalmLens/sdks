Long audio and video, rendered media and previews.

- `assets().list_timeline(ListTimeline { .. })` pages through every segment of an audio or video asset's findings timeline in time order, from `GET /v1/projects/{project_id}/assets/{asset_id}/timeline` in the client's project. Pass each page's `next_cursor` back unchanged; cursors are opaque. The blocking client offers it too.
- `classification.moderation.timeline` is `Some(AssetModerationTimeline { segment_count, .. })` for an audio or video asset with a findings timeline, and `None` otherwise. `classification.moderation.raw` no longer includes the timeline, and `MediaClassificationReport` is removed.
- `classification.progress` reports `completed_sections` and `total_sections` while long media is analysed in sections, and the `asset.processing-progress` event reports the same. The failure reasons include `media-limit` and `spend-limit`.
- `content.rendered` (`AssetRendered`) describes the asset's displayable file: its `kind` (`AssetRenderedKind::Image`, `Pdf`, `Video` or `Audio`), `format`, `width`, `height` and, for audio, waveform `peaks`. `content.preview` (`AssetPreview`) describes its still image: `width`, `height`, `thumbhash` and `average_color`. They replace `content.image` and `AssetImage`. The file's own `width`, `height`, `duration_seconds`, `page_count`, `perceptual_hash` and `perceptual_hash_quality` are on `content`, and `content.file_metadata` is now `content.metadata`.
- The CDN's new preview resource, `CdnResource::Preview`, serves the asset's still image: the image itself, a document's first page or slide, a website's first screen, a video's poster frame or an audio waveform. `cdn().get_asset_url`, `cdn().serve` and `assets().create_signed_url` (with `SignedUrlResource::Preview`) accept it, and it takes lenses and transforms. The original resource takes none, and the rendered resource takes them only when it is an image; the CDN answers `asset/invalid-transform` otherwise, and `asset/no-rendered-version` for a resource the asset doesn't have. `models::SIGNED_CDN_RESOURCES` lists the resources a signed URL can be created for.
- The mock client serves the timeline endpoint (empty, as the mock analyses no media), fills `content.rendered` and `content.preview` for each asset type, serves the preview and rendered resources as the CDN does, and gives mock assets a `None` progress and timeline.
- Audio and video can be up to 2 GiB when sent in parts, whether `assets().create` and `assets().classify` split a large file or you use the multipart part API; one request still takes up to 50 MiB. Before billing is set up, the API keeps every audio and video upload to 50 MiB and answers `upload/file-too-large`.
